Privacy Policy

ExamLegacy — Powered by SANJAYXLEGACY · Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023
Last updated: 28 September 2026

1. Who we are

ExamLegacy is a digital education platform that sells and delivers study material (PDF notes, PYQ compilations, formula kits) and provides an in-app AI study assistant. This policy explains what personal data we collect, why we collect it, and the rights you have under India's DPDP Act, 2023.

This notice is published in plain language as required by the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, together with the consent notices shown at the time of sign-in and payment.

2. Data we collect

  • Account data: Google account identifier, email address, display name and profile picture — shared by Google when you choose "Continue with Google". We never see your Google password.
  • Optional profile data: phone number you enter for payment reference; profile photo you upload.
  • Transaction data: order records, wallet balance and AI credit ledger entries. Card / UPI credentials are entered only on the Cashfree payment page — they never touch our servers.
  • Usage data: your Study AI conversations, support messages, notifications read state, and basic security logs (IP address, rate-limit counters) used to prevent abuse.
  • Documents you open: purchased PDFs are streamed with a watermark containing your name and email to deter redistribution.

We do not collect Aadhaar, PAN, biometric data, or the contents of your contacts. No personal data is sold or shared for advertising.

3. Why we use it (purposes)

  • To create your account, sign you in, and show your library, wallet and credits.
  • To process purchases, grant access to bought material, and prevent fraud or refund abuse.
  • To answer your Study AI / Support questions (your query text is sent to our AI provider to generate the answer).
  • To send transactional messages: purchase receipts, payment confirmations, support replies.
  • To keep the platform secure: rate limiting, abuse detection, audit logs of admin actions.

Legal bases: your consent (given at sign-in), performance of contract (delivering purchases), and legitimate interests limited to security and record-keeping.

4. Who we share it with

  • Cashfree Payments — to process payments and verify them server-side (name, email, amount, phone).
  • Google / Firebase — for sign-in, identity-token verification, and fraud prevention.
  • Google Gemini API — the text of your study questions only, to generate AI answers.
  • Our email provider (SMTP) — to deliver receipts and account mail.
  • Law enforcement — only when legally required under Indian law.

We do not publish PDF files at public URLs. Materials are delivered only inside the signed-in app through short-lived, watermarked sessions.

5. Retention & security

  • Account records are kept while your account exists; financial records are retained as required by Indian tax and accounting law.
  • AI conversations are kept to continue your chat history; you can request deletion.
  • Viewer sessions expire automatically (2 hours) and must be re-issued after sign-in.
  • Controls in use: HTTPS in transit, hashed session data, server-side secrets, prepared SQL statements, rate limiting, and least-privilege access.

6. Your rights (DPDP Act 2023)

  • Access — see the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correction & erasure — correct your name/phone, or ask us to delete your account and associated personal data (transaction records may be retained where the law requires).
  • Withdraw consent — stop optional processing (e.g. delete your account from Account → Privacy & Security, or by email).
  • Grievance redressal — we respond to privacy complaints within 30 days.

To exercise any right, email us below with the email address registered on your account so we can verify ownership.

7. Children

The platform is intended for users aged 18 and above. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.
